The Clockmaker of Arden Valley

"For forty years, Elias never owned a watch that ticked louder than the rain. Customers came from the city demanding chimes and alarms, yet he only handed them brass pendulums that swung at half the speed of an ordinary second. 'If you watch it closely enough,' Elias told them, 'the afternoon lasts twice as long.'"
